## Who I Am

I'm **Muhammed Safwan**, a Full Stack Developer based in **Bengaluru, Karnataka, India**. I specialize in building modern, scalable web applications using cutting-edge technologies like **Next.js**, **React**, **TypeScript**, **Node.js**, and **PostgreSQL**.

I started my web development journey in early 2024. What began as curiosity quickly turned into a serious pursuit. Through consistent practice, hands-on projects, and self-directed learning, I've built a strong foundation in full stack development — from crafting responsive front-end interfaces to designing robust back-end architectures.
