## WHO AM I

I'm a Full Stack Developer based in India. I focus on building modern, scalable web applications using modern technologies like Next.js, React.js, Node.js, and PostgreSQL.

I started my journey in web development in early 2024. What began as curiosity quickly turned into a serious pursuit. Without relying on formal courses, I’ve built my skills through consistent practice, hands-on projects, and self-learning. This has helped me develop a strong problem-solving mindset and the ability to learn new technologies independently.

I primarily work with modern tools and frameworks such as Next.js, PostgreSQL, and Tailwind CSS, and I use GitHub and Vercel to manage and deploy my projects efficiently.
